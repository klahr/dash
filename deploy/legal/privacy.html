Where it is

Everything above is on our own server at [hosting provider], in [country]. Your code is not: it stays on the computers you run tasks on.

Who else gets it

  • [Mail provider] sends our emails: it gets your address and the email.
  • Stripe, if your organization pays for a plan: your organization's name and its owner's email address. Stripe keeps the card, billing address and tax id; see Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Google, if you sign in with Google.
  • Your browser's push service (Google, Mozilla or Apple), if you turn on push notifications. What it carries is encrypted.
  • The webhooks you add get your tasks' events.
  • Anthropic: the service itself never sends your data to Anthropic. The computers you run tasks on send prompts and code to Anthropic through Claude Code, under your own Claude account and Anthropic's terms.

We don't sell your data. We use no analytics or tracking, and our pages load nothing from other sites.

Cookies

We use only the cookies the service needs to work: dash_session keeps you signed in (7 days), dash_google_… and dash_glink_… hold a Google sign-in in progress (10 minutes), and dash_preview opens live previews (12 hours). The app keeps its view settings in your browser's local storage.

Your rights

You can:

  • Get a copy of what we keep about you, in a file you can take elsewhere: Account → Your data → Download.
  • Delete your account: Account → Your data → Delete account…. Tasks and messages you wrote in other people's organizations stay there, without your name.
  • Ask us to correct your email address or username.
  • Ask us to restrict or object to what we do with your data, and withdraw consent you gave (turn notifications off), at any time.
  • Complain to a data protection authority, e.g. [the one where you live, or ours: name and website].

Write to [help@example.com] for anything you can't do yourself. We answer within a month.

Changes

When we change this policy we update the date above, and tell you by email before a change that matters takes effect.
