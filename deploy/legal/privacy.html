You don't have to give us any of this, but without an email address and a password (or a Google account) we can't give you an account.

We make no decisions about you by automated means alone, and we don't use your data for advertising or profiling. The service isn't meant for children under [16].

Your organization's content

What an organization puts in the service (its projects, tasks, chats and attachments) is the organization's. For that content the organization decides what is done with it (it is the controller) and we process it for the organization (we are its processor, under the data processing terms in our terms of service). If you are a member, ask its owners about it first; we help them answer. This policy covers what we decide ourselves: your account, signing in, security and billing.

In an organization, the other members see your username, email address, profile picture and what you do there. Its owners and admins also manage your membership and can sign you out of it. The people who run the service (platform admins) can see every account, to run and support it.

How we keep it safe

Passwords are hashed with Argon2id, and tokens and sign-in cookies are kept only as hashes. The service is served over HTTPS only, offers two-factor sign-in, and only the people who run it can reach its server. [Backups are encrypted.]

Where it is

Everything above is on our own server at [hosting provider], in [country]. Your code is not: it stays on the computers you run tasks on.

Who else gets it

  • [Hosting provider] runs the server the service is on[, and keeps our backups].
  • [Mail provider] sends our emails (confirmation and reset links, and the notifications and summaries you turn on): it gets your address and the email.
  • Stripe, if your organization pays for a plan: your organization's name and its owner's email address. At checkout you give Stripe the card, billing address and VAT number; it handles the payment, checks it for fraud, and keeps the invoices. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Google, if you sign in with Google.
  • Your browser's push service (Google, Mozilla or Apple), if you turn on push notifications. What it carries is encrypted.
  • The webhooks you add get your tasks' events.
  • Anthropic: the service itself never sends your data to Anthropic. The computers you run tasks on send prompts and code to Anthropic through Claude Code, under your own Claude account and Anthropic's terms. That includes what the service asks your own runner to do for you: naming and summarising tasks, and the short spoken summaries if you turn them on.
  • Your browser's speech recognition, if you turn on dictation or voice commands: the browser does it, not the service. Chrome sends the audio to Google; Safari may do it on the device. We only get the text you send.

We don't sell your data. We use no analytics or tracking, and our pages load nothing from other sites.

Cookies

We use only the cookies the service needs to work: __Host-dash_session keeps you signed in (7 days), dash_google_… and dash_glink_… hold a Google sign-in in progress (10 minutes), and dash_preview opens live previews (12 hours). The app keeps its view settings in your browser's local storage.

Your rights

You can:

  • Get a copy of what we keep about you, in a file you can take elsewhere: Account → Profile → Download.
  • Delete your account: Account → Profile → Delete account…. Tasks and messages you wrote in other people's organizations stay there, without your name.
  • Ask us to correct your email address or username.
  • Ask us to restrict or object to what we do with your data, and withdraw consent you gave (turn notifications off), at any time.
  • Complain to a data protection authority, e.g. [the one where you live, or ours: name and website].

Write to [help@example.com] for anything you can't do yourself. We answer within a month.
