## How to sign

Open a pull request. The CLA assistant comments on it; reply with this exact comment:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your GitHub username, the date and the pull request are recorded in
`signatures/cla.json` on the repository's `cla-signatures` branch. You sign once; it covers
all your later pull requests too.
